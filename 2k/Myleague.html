My League Eras

In NBA 2K27, you can start your MyCAREER or MyLeague in one of five historical eras: Magic vs. Bird, Jordan, Kobe, LeBron, or Steph. Each with period accurate rosters, rules, and presentations.

Magic vs. Bird Era (1984)
Begins with Michael Jordan’s rookie season on the Bulls, in the height of the Celtics vs Lakers rivalry
Jordan Era (1991)
Start of the Bulls’ first three peat
Kobe Era (2003)
Kobe’s prime, alongside a historic draft class and LeBron’s entry
LeBron Era (2010)
Superteams dominate as LeBron’s Heat era begins
Steph Era (2016)
The 3‑point revolution and modern NBA style
